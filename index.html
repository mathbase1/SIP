Calculating review date…

The review starts at 14 calendar days after issue and moves forward to the next weekday in college term time when needed.

The issue date is set automatically when you generate the texts.

Calendar dates & review rules

14 calendar days, then the next eligible weekday. Reviews skip weekends, student holidays, bank holidays, college closures and staff-only days. The review is never brought forward.

Eligible student review windows, Monday to Friday only
Teaching blockEligible date range

Other excluded dates: Monday 3 May 2027 is a bank holiday. Monday 9 November 2026, Friday 18 December 2026 and Monday 12 April 2027 are staff-development days, already outside the windows above.

November note: 2–6 November 2026 is an unnumbered exam week in the supplied calendar; normal teaching is not confirmed, so automatic reviews skip it.

Source: College Calendar 2026–27 FINAL.pdf, embedded in your supplied calendar. The last supported review day is Friday 2 July 2027. Future academic-year dates must be added to this app before it can schedule later reviews.

These dates follow the general student calendar. They do not book a meeting or check an individual timetable.
